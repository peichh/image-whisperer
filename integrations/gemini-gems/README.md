# Gemini Gems (work and school accounts, or until your Gems are retired)

Gems take **instructions** plus optional **knowledge files**. Source: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780

## Create the Gem

1. Gemini → **Gems** → **New Gem**.
2. Name: `Image Whisperer`.
3. Instructions: paste everything under the `---` line of `integrations/standalone/image-whisperer-instructions.md`.
4. Knowledge → Add files (optional, makes answers more precise): `adapters/gemini.md`, `core/image-editing.md`, `knowledge/anti-ai-appearance.md`, `core/aesthetic-translation.md`, `knowledge/anti-generic-aesthetics.md`, `knowledge/photography.md`, `styles/thai-art.md`, `styles/graphic-design.md`.
5. Save, then try: "ขอพรอมต์ภาพก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ สำหรับโพสต์เฟซบุ๊ก".

Personal accounts: Google converts Gems into skills; prefer the skill zip in `gemini-app/`.

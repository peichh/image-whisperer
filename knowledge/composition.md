# Composition

Decide framing from the purpose, then say it in a few words.

## Purpose → composition

| Purpose | Composition |
|---|---|
| social feed post | 4:5 or 1:1, subject large and clear |
| story / reel / TikTok cover | 9:16, subject in the middle band, space top/bottom for UI and text |
| YouTube thumbnail | 16:9, one big face or object, high contrast, space for 2-4 big words |
| website hero / banner | wide (16:9 to 21:9), subject on one side, clean space for headline on the other |
| poster | 2:3 or 3:4, clear hierarchy: visual → title → details |
| product listing | 1:1, product centred, plain or simple background, entire product visible |
| slide image | 16:9, simple, lots of clean space |
| phone wallpaper | 9:19.5 or 9:16, subject away from clock area at the top |
| print / A4 | 1:√2 (about 5:7 or 3:4 as closest common ratio) |

## Tools

- Rule of thirds for natural balance; centred composition for symmetry, products, icons.
- Leading lines (roads, rails, rivers) towards the subject.
- Foreground framing (doorways, leaves) for depth.
- Negative space: say where it is ("empty sky in the top third for text").
- Groups: arrange in a line, a triangle or a loose cluster; vary heights; nobody fully hidden.
- Keep important things away from edges where platforms crop.

## Say it compactly

"Vertical 9:16, the girl in the lower centre, empty pink sky above for a title."

# Lighting

Describe light by **source, direction, quality, colour**. One sentence is usually enough.

## Sources and their look

| Source | Quality | Colour | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| midday sun | hard, short shadows from above | neutral-white | outdoor daytime, tropical street |
| golden hour (low sun) | warm, long shadows, rim light | warm orange | romance, nostalgia, travel (do not default to it) |
| blue hour | soft, low contrast | cool blue + warm artificial lights | city evenings |
| overcast sky | very soft, almost no shadows | neutral-cool | portraits, products, realistic everyday |
| window light | soft, directional, falls off across the room | depends on sky | interiors, food, portraits |
| tungsten / warm bulbs | soft-hard | warm amber | homes, restaurants at night |
| fluorescent / LED panel | flat, sometimes greenish | cool | offices, convenience stores, hospitals |
| neon / signs | coloured, small sources | pink, cyan, red… | night streets, nightlife |
| screen glow | soft, close to face | blue-white | gaming, night phone use |
| on-camera flash | hard, flat front light, dark falloff behind | neutral | party snapshots, 90s/2000s look |
| studio softbox | soft, controlled | neutral | product, beauty, headshots |
| candle / fire | warm, flickering, strong falloff | deep orange | intimate, ceremonies |

## Direction words

front light (flat, few shadows) · side light (shape, texture) · back light / rim light (outline, glow) · top light (harsh, eye shadows) · under light (eerie).

## Mood mapping (use only if mood is requested)

- calm, honest, everyday → soft overcast or window light
- warm, nostalgic → late-afternoon warm light, gentle haze
- premium product → soft key, clean gradient background, controlled reflections
- dramatic → hard side light, deep shadows
- mysterious → low key, single small source, haze

## Consistency

- All shadows point away from the same key light.
- Indoor + window: exterior visible through window should be bright, interior a little darker unless lit.
- Mixed light (warm interior + blue window) is realistic and attractive, but say it on purpose.
- Night: name the light source in the scene ("lit by a street lamp on the right").

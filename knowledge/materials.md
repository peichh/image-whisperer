# Materials

Name the material and one or two visible properties. Use for close-ups, products, food and realism.

| Material | Visible properties to mention |
|---|---|
| skin | natural texture, pores, fine lines, slight redness, sheen on forehead in heat |
| hair | strands, flyaways, shine follows light direction |
| cotton | matte, soft wrinkles |
| denim | twill texture, fading at seams |
| silk / satin | smooth highlights, fluid folds |
| wool / knit | visible stitches, fuzzy surface |
| leather | fine grain, creases, soft sheen |
| metal (brushed) | linear highlights; (polished) mirror reflections of surroundings |
| glass | transparency, refraction, edge highlights, reflections |
| plastic | uniform sheen, slight scratches when used |
| ceramic | glossy glaze or matte, small imperfections in handmade pieces |
| wood | grain direction, knots, wear on edges |
| stone / concrete | porous, stains, rough texture |
| paper | fibres, folds, slight curl |
| water / liquid | transparency, surface reflections, droplets, condensation on cold cups |
| food | moisture, steam, glossy sauce, crisp edges, crumbs |
| Thai items | e.g. banana leaf (waxy, ribbed), rattan/woven bamboo, lacquerware, Thai silk (iridescent sheen), clay pots |

Product rule: describe the real product's material accurately (glossy vs matte, label paper vs printed). Do not invent extra materials, logos or finishes.

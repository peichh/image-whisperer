# Photography

Use camera vocabulary only for photographic images, and only terms that change the picture. Do not attach camera settings to every prompt.

## Choose the camera "look" from the purpose

| Purpose | Look | Useful words |
|---|---|---|
| casual / social / family | smartphone | "a candid smartphone photo", "phone wide main camera", "natural phone HDR" — see `styles/smartphone.md` |
| documentary / street | 35 mm prime, available light | "35mm street photo", "natural available light", "slight grain" |
| portrait / headshot | 85 mm, shallow depth of field | "85mm portrait", "soft background blur", "eye-level" |
| product / e-commerce | studio, controlled light | "clean studio product photo", "softbox lighting", "seamless white background" |
| food | 50-100 mm, close, soft side light | "overhead flat lay" or "45-degree angle", "soft window light" |
| architecture / interior | wide, straight verticals | "wide-angle interior photo, vertical lines straight", "natural daylight" |
| landscape | wide or telephoto | "wide landscape", "telephoto compression of distant mountains" |
| sports / wildlife | telephoto, fast shutter | "telephoto", "frozen action", "background compressed and blurred" |
| vintage / nostalgia | film stock or old camera | "35mm film photo, Kodak Portra 400 colours, fine grain", "1990s point-and-shoot flash photo" |

## Terms and what they do

- **Shot size**: extreme close-up, close-up, medium shot, cowboy shot, full body, wide shot.
- **Angle**: eye level (neutral), low angle (power, scale), high angle (small, vulnerable), top-down/flat lay, over-the-shoulder, dutch angle (tension, use rarely).
- **Focal length feel**: 14-24 mm ultra-wide (space, distortion); 24-35 mm wide/normal (context); 50 mm (natural); 85-135 mm (portrait, compression); 200 mm+ (strong compression).
- **Depth of field**: "shallow depth of field" (subject sharp, background soft); "deep focus, everything sharp" (landscapes, architecture, groups).
- **Shutter feel**: "motion blur on the passing cars", "frozen splash".
- **Flash**: "direct on-camera flash" (party, snapshot, hard shadow behind); "bounced flash" (soft).
- **Film/sensor feel**: grain, halation, colour cast of named film stocks; sensor noise in low light for phones.

Exact f-numbers and ISO rarely help image models; prefer the visible effect ("background softly blurred"). Some models (FLUX.2, Gemini) respond to camera and lens names; use them when the look matters.

## Realism signals

- Imperfect framing for candid shots; slight tilt; cropped elements at the edges.
- Real-world clutter appropriate to the place.
- Lens behaviour: mild vignetting, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges (only for realism goals, sparingly).

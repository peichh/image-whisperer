# Anime

## When

Anime, Japanese animation, อนิเมะ, cel-shaded characters, key visuals, fan art in anime style.

## Key traits

- Clean line art, cel shading with 1-2 shadow tones, large expressive eyes, simplified noses and mouths.
- Background art may be painterly and detailed while characters stay flat-shaded.
- Sub-styles: 90s cel anime (grain, softer palette), modern TV anime (crisp, bright), theatrical film look (detailed light, atmospheric skies), chibi (2-3 heads tall).

## Prompt words

"anime illustration", "cel-shaded", "clean line art", "anime key visual", "90s anime screencap style", "detailed painted background"

## Avoid

- photorealistic
- skin pores
- DSLR, lens names
- 8K photo

# Anime

## When

Anime, Japanese animation, อนิเมะ, cel-shaded characters, key visuals, fan art in anime style.

## Key traits

- Clean line art, cel shading with 1-2 shadow tones, large expressive eyes, simplified noses and mouths.
- Background art may be painterly and detailed while characters stay flat-shaded.
- Sub-styles: 90s cel anime (grain, softer palette), modern TV anime (crisp, bright), theatrical film look (detailed light, atmospheric skies), chibi (2-3 heads tall).

- [I, owner-observed on Gemini] "anime" alone can drift to a Western comic / graphic-novel look (thick ink outlines, solid black shadows, realistic Western faces) or to mobile-game splash art. State the anime traits and one contrast phrase: "modern Japanese TV anime, not a Western comic or game illustration — thin clean line art, flat two-tone cel shading, anime facial proportions, no heavy solid black shadows".

- [I, owner-observed] Avoid "ink" in anime line descriptions ("dark ink line art" pulls toward comic inking); say "thin clean line art".

## Prompt words

"anime illustration", "cel-shaded", "clean line art", "anime key visual", "90s anime screencap style", "detailed painted background"

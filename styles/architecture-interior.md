# Architecture and interiors

## When

Architecture, building, house, interior design, room, ตกแต่งบ้าน, ห้อง.

## Key traits

- Straight vertical lines (two-point perspective, camera level).
- Real materials and scale cues (doors 2 m high, furniture sizes).
- Light: natural daylight through windows, warm practical lamps at dusk.

## Prompt words

"architectural photo, wide-angle, vertical lines corrected", "interior photo in soft natural daylight", "Scandinavian / Japandi / modern tropical style"

## Avoid

- fish-eye distortion
- impossible structures (unless concept art)
- too many styles mixed

## Model notes

For redesign of a real room use EDIT: keep walls, windows, layout and camera; change furniture/finishes only.

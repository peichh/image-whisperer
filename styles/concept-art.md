# Concept art and environment design

## When

Concept art, environment design, game art, keyframe, คอนเซ็ปต์อาร์ต.

## Key traits

- Readable big shapes, clear focal point, atmospheric depth, painterly finish.
- Environment: scale figure for size; mood through light and weather.

## Prompt words

"environment concept art, painterly digital", "tiny human figure for scale", "atmospheric perspective"

## Avoid

- over-rendered tiny details everywhere

## Model notes

Use 16:9 or 21:9 for environments; specify the time of day and weather.

# Korean and Chinese illustration styles

## When

Korean webtoon, Korean illustration, Chinese ink painting, guofeng, gongbi, ภาพแนวจีน/เกาหลี.

## Key traits

- Korean webtoon / Korean illustration: clean digital line, soft gradients, pastel palettes, slender fashionable characters, vertical scrolling layouts.
- Chinese ink wash (shuimo): monochrome ink, varied brush pressure, empty space, mountains/mist, seals.
- Gongbi: fine precise outlines, flat delicate colours, silk-painting look.
- Guofeng (modern Chinese style): flowing hanfu, ornate hair pieces, rich reds and golds, painterly digital.

## Prompt words

"Chinese ink wash painting on rice paper", "gongbi fine-line painting", "modern guofeng digital illustration", "Korean webtoon style, clean lines, soft cel shading"

## Avoid

- mixing Japanese, Chinese and Korean motifs by accident

# Fantasy

## When

Fantasy, magic, dragons, elves, myth, แฟนตาซี, Himmapan creatures.

## Key traits

- Coherent world rules: one magic look, consistent architecture and costume culture.
- Scale cues for big creatures; light from the magic itself.
- Thai mythology: naga, garuda, kinnaree, Himmapan forest — describe visually.

## Prompt words

"epic fantasy illustration", "painterly digital art", "glowing runes casting blue light on the stone"

## Avoid

- generic medieval Europe when the user asked for another culture

## Model notes

Midjourney is strong for painterly fantasy; Gemini/FLUX for controlled composition.

# Fashion photography and lookbooks

## When

Fashion shoot, lookbook, outfit, OOTD, แฟชั่น, ชุด.

## Key traits

- Purpose decides look: e-commerce (plain background, full outfit visible, neutral pose), lookbook (lifestyle setting), editorial (strong concept, bold light), street style (candid, city).

## Prompt words

"full-body e-commerce fashion photo on a plain light-grey background", "street-style photo", "editorial fashion shoot with hard side light"

## Avoid

- changing the garment's cut, colour or logo in edit/reference work
- unrealistic body proportions unless requested

## Model notes

For real garments use REFERENCE mode and preserve the product exactly.

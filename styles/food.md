# Food photography

## When

Food, menu, อาหาร, drinks, dessert, recipe image.

## Key traits

- Angle: overhead flat lay for bowls and spreads; 45-degree for plated dishes; eye-level for drinks and burgers.
- Soft side or back light shows texture and steam; avoid flat front flash (except for casual phone looks).
- Real cues: steam, crumbs, sauce drips, condensation, garnish appropriate to the dish.

## Prompt words

"overhead food photo", "45-degree angle", "soft window back light, gentle steam", "on a banana leaf on a rustic wooden table"

## Avoid

- wrong ingredients for named dishes
- plastic-looking glossy food

## Model notes

Name the dish, then describe what it looks like ("pad kra pao: stir-fried minced pork with holy basil, a crispy fried egg on rice").

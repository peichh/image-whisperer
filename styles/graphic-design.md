# Graphic design: posters, logos, infographics, social graphics

## When

Poster, logo, banner, infographic, slide, social media graphic, menu, โปสเตอร์, โลโก้, อินโฟกราฟิก.

## Key traits

- Hierarchy: headline → visual → supporting text → call to action.
- Exact text in quotes; short; specify font feel (bold sans-serif, handwritten, Thai rounded loop-less font) and placement.
- Logos: simple shapes, 1-3 colours, works small; say 'flat vector logo on a white background'.
- Infographics: list sections in order, with the exact numbers/labels; fewer words render better.

## Prompt words

"flat vector logo", "minimal poster with bold sans-serif headline \"…\" at the top", "infographic with four labelled sections: …"

## Avoid

- long paragraphs of text
- made-up statistics
- too many fonts

## Model notes

Best text rendering: Gemini 3 image models, Qwen-Image, Seedream 4+, FLUX.2/3, Z-Image (EN/ZH). Thai text is less reliable on all models; keep it very short or leave space and add text later. Gemini docs: generating the text first then asking for the image works best.

# Illustration (general, children's book, editorial, flat)

## When

Illustration, drawing, ภาพวาด, ภาพประกอบ, children's book art, editorial illustration, flat vector.

## Key traits

- Pick one medium: flat vector, digital painting, gouache, coloured pencil, ink and wash, paper cut-out.
- Children's books: soft shapes, warm palettes, readable silhouettes, simple backgrounds.
- Editorial: a clear visual idea or metaphor, limited palette, bold shapes.

## Prompt words

"flat vector illustration", "gouache children's book illustration", "editorial illustration with a limited palette of …", "soft textured brushwork"

## Avoid

- photo/camera terms
- mixed media unless asked

## Model notes

State the medium first. Midjourney excels at painterly looks; Gemini/Qwen at illustrations with text.

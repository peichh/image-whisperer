# Manga and comics

## When

Manga, comic panels, black-and-white comic, webtoon, มังงะ, การ์ตูนช่อง.

## Key traits

- Black ink line art with screentone shading (manga) or full colour vertical panels (webtoon).
- Panels need a layout: number of panels, reading direction, what happens in each.
- Speech bubbles with short quoted text; Thai text may render poorly — offer blank bubbles for adding text later.

## Prompt words

"black-and-white manga page", "screentone shading", "ink line art", "four-panel comic, read left to right", "empty speech bubbles"

## Avoid

- photorealistic
- colour unless requested
- too many panels (keep 1-6)

## Model notes

Gemini and Qwen-Image handle multi-panel layouts and text better than SD-based models. Describe panels as a numbered list.

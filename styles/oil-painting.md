# Oil and acrylic painting

## When

Oil painting, สีน้ำมัน, classical portrait, impressionist, palette knife.

## Key traits

- Visible brushstrokes, impasto texture, rich colour blending, canvas weave.
- Name the tradition rather than a living artist: Dutch Golden Age chiaroscuro, French impressionism, academic realism.

## Prompt words

"oil painting on canvas", "visible impasto brushstrokes", "impressionist palette, broken colour", "chiaroscuro lighting in the manner of Dutch Golden Age portraits"

## Avoid

- camera terms
- glossy digital rendering

## Model notes

Historic artists in public domain are fine to name; for living artists, describe traits.

# Product photography and packshots

## When

Product photo, packshot, สินค้า, e-commerce, ad for a product, mockup.

## Key traits

- Product is hero: fully visible, accurate shape and label, correct scale.
- Studio: seamless background, softbox key, controlled reflections, contact shadow.
- Lifestyle: product in use in a plausible setting; props support, never hide, the product.

## Prompt words

"clean studio product photo", "seamless off-white background", "soft diffused key light from the left", "soft contact shadow", "label facing the camera"

## Avoid

- invented logos or text on the product
- extra products
- warped labels

## Model notes

Real products: use the product image as a reference and say 'keep the product exactly the same'. Gemini 3 Pro Image / Qwen-Image-Edit / Seedream / FLUX 3 support multi-reference compositing.

# Science fiction

## When

Sci-fi, futuristic, cyberpunk, space, robots, ไซไฟ.

## Key traits

- Pick a sub-genre: hard sci-fi (functional, NASA-like), cyberpunk (neon, rain, dense signage), solarpunk (green, bright), retro-futurism.
- Technology should look usable: panels, wear, cables, scale.

## Prompt words

"cyberpunk street at night, wet asphalt reflecting neon signs", "hard science fiction spacecraft interior, functional panels"

## Avoid

- neon in every sci-fi image
- random glowing lines

## Model notes

Signage text: quote short real text or say 'signs with unreadable graphics' to avoid gibberish.

# Smartphone and casual photos

## When

Requests that say phone, mobile, candid, selfie, vlog, everyday, ถ่ายมือถือ, รูปธรรมดา, or posts for friends and family.

## Key traits

- Wide main camera (about 24-28 mm equivalent) or front camera for selfies (wide, arm's-length distortion).
- Most of the scene in focus; portrait mode gives an artificial-looking soft background with slight edge errors.
- Computational HDR: bright skies kept, shadows lifted, a little over-sharpened; low light gets noise and smoothing.
- Candid framing: slightly tilted, subject not perfectly centred, cropped edges, real clutter.
- Available light only, or harsh direct phone flash at night.
- Who holds the phone matters: a friend a few metres away at chest height (natural proportions, candid moment), arm's-length selfie (wide distortion), or the phone held high and close (big head, short legs — avoid unless that is the look).
- Selfies: one hand holds the phone. Name which hand ("holding the phone in her left hand at arm's length") and give any pose only the other hand; never assign both hands plus a selfie.
- Portrait-orientation 3:4 is the native phone shape; use 9:16 for stories.

## Prompt words

"a candid smartphone photo", "taken on a phone", "phone's wide main camera", "front-camera selfie at arm's length", "natural phone HDR", "slight noise in the shadows", "direct phone flash"

## Avoid

- studio lighting, softboxes
- 85mm portrait bokeh
- cinematic
- model-like posing
- perfect symmetry

## Model notes

Gemini and FLUX respond well to 'smartphone photo' wording. Midjourney: add `--raw` to reduce house style. SDXL: 'amateur photo, phone photo' works better as early tokens.

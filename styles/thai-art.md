# Thai art and Thai visual culture

## When

Thai art, ลายไทย, จิตรกรรมฝาผนัง, Thai mural, Thai festivals, Thai costume, Thai food and places.

## Key traits

- Thai mural painting (จิตรกรรมฝาผนังไทย): flat perspective, figures in graceful poses, gold and deep red, zig-zag dividing lines (sinthao), no cast shadows.
- Lai Thai patterns (ลายกนก): flame-like flowing motifs, gold on red or black lacquer.
- Modern Thai: street food stalls, shophouses, songthaews, tuk-tuks, 7-Eleven, temple roofs with chofa finials, Thai script signage.
- Traditional dress: chut thai (e.g. chut thai chakkri with a sabai shoulder cloth), pha sin skirts, sompak; describe visibly because model knowledge varies.

## Prompt words

"traditional Thai mural painting style, flat perspective, gold and deep red palette", "Lai Thai kanok pattern", "Bangkok shophouse street", "Thai script shop signs"

## Avoid

- generic 'Asian' mixes (Chinese lanterns, Japanese torii) unless asked
- lightening skin tones
- random fake Thai text — quote real text or ask for blank signs

## Model notes

Always add a short English description of Thai-specific items. Thai text: keep short, quoted; Gemini and Qwen-Image are usually better at non-Latin scripts than SD-based models, but results vary — offer to leave text space blank.

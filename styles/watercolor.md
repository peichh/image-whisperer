# Watercolour

## When

Watercolour, สีน้ำ.

## Key traits

- Transparent washes, soft edges, wet-on-wet blooms, visible paper texture, white of the paper as highlights.
- Limited detail; loose edges; pigment pooling at edges of washes.

## Prompt words

"watercolour painting on textured cold-press paper", "soft wet-on-wet washes", "loose brushwork", "white paper showing through"

## Avoid

- sharp digital edges
- heavy black outlines (unless ink and wash)
- photorealism

## Model notes

All models handle this well; keep subject description simple.
